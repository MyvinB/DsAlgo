# Blind 75 — 3-Week Spaced Repetition Study Plan

**Start:** May 2, 2026 | **End:** June 3, 2026

## Daily Workflow
1. Ask me: **"What's my assignment today?"**
2. I give you ~5 new problems + review problems due today
3. After each problem, tell me: **"Done with LC X, rating Y"**
   - `1` = Easy, no hints | `2` = Some thought | `3` = Struggled/hints | `4` = Failed
4. I update the tracker and schedule your next review

## Spaced Repetition
- **Rating 1-2:** Review in 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 days
- **Rating 3:** Review in 1 → 1 → 3 → 7 days (compressed)
- **Rating 4:** Review next day until improved

## 3-Week Structure
| Week | Days | Focus |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 | 1-7 | 35 new problems + first reviews |
| 2 | 8-14 | 35 new problems + growing reviews |
| 3 | 15-21 | Final 5 new + heavy review of all 75 |
